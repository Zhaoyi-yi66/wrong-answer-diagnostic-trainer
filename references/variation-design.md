# Variation Design

Generate practice that tests and repairs the diagnosed cause. Merely replacing numbers is not enough.

## Practice ladder

### 1. Same-structure

Keep the mathematical structure and representation stable. Change only values. Use this to confirm the corrected procedure.

### 2. Contrast

Pair items that look similar but require different choices. Ask the learner to state why the method changes. Contrast is essential for operation selection, symbol meaning, regrouping conditions, and order of operations.

### 3. Transfer

Preserve the target concept while changing one surface feature: wording, orientation, missing-value position, representation, or context. Do not change several dimensions at once.

### 4. Delayed review

Reuse the concept after a gap without copying the same item. Default schedule:

- next day: one same-structure plus one contrast item;
- 3–7 days later: one mixed or transfer item.

## Difficulty dimensions

Control these independently:

- number magnitude;
- regrouping count;
- number of steps;
- presence of distractor information;
- operation choice;
- representation: equation, vertical form, diagram, or story;
- unknown position;
- language complexity;
- time pressure.

Early remediation should change at most one major dimension per stage. Do not use time pressure until accuracy is stable.

## Grade and scope checks

- Do not introduce negative numbers, decimals, fractions, unknown symbols, or multi-step operations unless the grade/context allows them.
- Keep division exact unless remainders are the target.
- In word problems, use familiar, culturally neutral contexts and remove irrelevant reading load unless reading is itself the diagnosed issue.
- Avoid trick questions and answer patterns that allow guessing.
- Ensure generated choices, if any, correspond to plausible error mechanisms rather than random numbers.

## Verification checklist

For every item:

1. Solve it independently.
2. Confirm one intended answer.
3. Confirm the item actually exposes or practises the diagnosed cause.
4. Confirm the notation and vocabulary are consistent.
5. Confirm the learner page contains no answer leak.
